RECIPE SHEET

How to fill the Banega recipe sheet

One row per dish. Every row becomes a recipe card in the app, with its ingredients, steps in English and Hindi, and a photo. The Banega team imports the sheet as it is, so please keep the column names and the formats below.

The four rules that matter most

1
One row per dishDo not merge cells or add blank rows between dishes.
2
Use | to separate itemsIngredients, steps, tags and slots are lists. Put a | between items, never a new line.
3
Same number of steps in both languagesStep 3 in English must be step 3 in Hindi. The app reads them out step by step.
4
Save as CSV UTF-8In Excel: File → Save As → "CSV UTF-8". In Google Sheets: File → Download → CSV. This keeps Hindi text intact.

Columns

The first 21 columns are imported into the app. The last 4 are for the review only and are ignored by the import.

Ingredients and photos

Writing ingredients

  • Use the everyday name: Pyaaz, Tamatar, Adrak-lahsun, Hari mirch.
  • Write two ingredients in one line with a hyphen: "Adrak-lahsun 1 tbsp".
  • The first ingredient is the main one. The app uses it for "don't like" and swap suggestions.
  • Mark skippable items with (optional): "Dhaniya (optional)". The cook can then leave them out when they run short.
  • If an ingredient is new to Banega, the import lists it and the team adds it. Nothing is lost.

Photos

  • Landscape 4:3, at least 1600 × 1200 pixels, JPG or WebP under 1 MB.
  • The cooked dish in a katori or thali, from about 45°, in daylight. No text or watermark.
  • The link must open the image file itself. A Google Drive or Dropbox share page will not work.
  • Links to example.com are treated as "no photo yet", so the app shows its drawn placeholder until a real link is added.
  • If you cannot host photos, send the files named by slug (moong-dal-khichdi.jpg) and leave image_url blank. The team uploads them in the admin panel.

Safety checks we rely on you for

  • List every ingredient, including garnish, tadka and oil. Allergies such as peanut, cashew, sesame, milk and gluten are detected from this list.
  • Mark Jain-safe only when you are sure; ginger, potato and onion rule it out.
  • Mark vrat only for ingredients allowed while fasting.
